# Requirements Evidence Pack

Use this to collect honest evidence for Chapter 1. Do not invent responses.

Use `interview_response_template.md` once for each participant. Identify people
only by codes such as S1, S2 and T1. Do not record names, student numbers or other
unnecessary personal information.

## Suggested Participants

- 4-6 students who may need to find a final year project supervisor.
- 1-2 staff/tutor perspectives if possible.

## Student Questions

1. How would you currently find a supervisor or project idea?
2. What information would you expect on a staff profile before contacting them?
3. How would you prefer to search or browse for a suitable supervisor?
4. What would make you confident that a project is suitable for you?
5. What would frustrate you in a supervisor/project browsing system?
6. What information about project availability would help you decide?
7. What filters/search options would be most useful?
8. Is there anything else the system should do or avoid?

## Staff Questions

1. What profile information would you want students to see?
2. How often would you need to update project ideas?
3. What fields should a project idea include?
4. Should project ideas show status or capacity?
5. What would make profile maintenance quick enough to use?
6. What errors or outdated information would concern you most?
7. What information should not be displayed publicly?
8. Is there anything else the system should do or avoid?

## Themes To Look For

- discoverability/search
- clarity of staff expertise
- clarity of project expectations
- project availability/capacity
- ease of staff maintenance
- confidence before contact

Do not force answers into these themes. Add new themes when the interview evidence
shows a recurring point that is not represented here.

Send back:
